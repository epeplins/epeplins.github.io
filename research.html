Past Work

Both of these projects were done during my undergrad career at the University of Michigan. I was advised by Emily Rauscher and Thomas Kennedy.

Are even 1D planets 3D?

JWST has allowed us greater sensitivity to exoplanet atmospheres than ever before, and this invites a re-evaluation of our assumptions in spectral modeling. In this project, we investigated the importance of limb effects — darkening and brightening — in hot Jupiter emission spectra. We did so through analysis of multiple different ray geometries with both 1D and 3D planet models, ultimately comparing the error produced by geometries that do not correctly capture limb effects to JWST precision. We found that there are potentially meaningful differences produced by these various geometries, and a paper discussing this investigation and its results is currently being revised for re-submission.

  • Lightning talk, presented at the 2023 Great Lakes Exoplanet Area Meeting
  • Poster, presented at the 2024 University of Michigan Astronomy Undergraduate Research Symposium
  • Paper, in revision
