As a rocky planet enthusiast, it probably comes as no surprise that I absolutely love collecting and learning about rocks, minerals, gems, etcetera. My eventual plan for this page is to compile what I've learned as well as some of my favorites from my collection, but that will have to come when I'm less busy!

My favorite rocks: petoskey stones; fluorite; anything that has cubic formation, really. I love when it's cubes. Not a rock, but I also really love finding lake glass — there's so much you can do with it!
